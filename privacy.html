Privacy Policy

Money Made Simple Publisher · Effective November 13, 2026

Money Made Simple Publisher (“the App”) is a private, internal tool run by the creator of the Money Made Simple channel. Its only job is to upload short finance-education videos that we make ourselves to our own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and no other people sign in to it.

What data the App accesses

  • Basic account info (TikTok user.info.basic): the display name and open ID of our own TikTok account, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Posting permissions (video.upload, video.publish): used only to upload our own video files and their captions to our own account.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok after we log in.

How the data is used and stored

  • Tokens are stored only on our own computer, in a local file that is never shared, published or committed to source control.
  • We do not collect, sell, share, or analyse any data about TikTok viewers or other users.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.
  • No data is sent to any third party other than TikTok’s own API.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as the App is in use. You can revoke the App’s access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; we delete the local token file when access is revoked or the App is retired.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children’s data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
